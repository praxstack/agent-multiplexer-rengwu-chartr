# Historical archive

These records preserve their original claims, dates, proposals, screenshots and
publication receipts. They do not certify the current application. The
[feature map](../features/index.md) describes the migration's current source baseline.

- [Research and audits](research/index.md): the full dated collection, including
  rejected prototypes and removed rich-chat behavior.
- [Workspace rewrite specification](workspace-rewrite-spec.md): preserved original
  product/design contract, retired as the active specification. Its 97 numbered
  stories map to [current feature records](../features/story-crosswalk.md).
- [Companion protocol](companion-protocol.md): retained inactive protocol; Companion
  is excluded from the current desktop build.
- [Architecture decisions](../decisions/adr/index.md): preserved identities and
  rationale, including supersession of the hosted plugin tier by ADR 0007.

Links and presentation were adapted during migration; original attachment bytes
are retained. The [migration manifest](../evidence/2026-10-05-docs-migration/migration-manifest.json)
records source/destination paths and original hashes. No publication was repeated,
and external research was not refreshed.
